Bus 1531 SKIP stickers

Nobody from your stop riding the AM bus? Send your stop's flashing SKIP sticker in the group.

  1. Find your stop's sticker in the bus group. All 11 are posted there.
  2. Tap it, then tap Add to Favorites. You only do this once.
  3. To skip: open the sticker panel, tap the ★ tab, tap your sticker. Done.
