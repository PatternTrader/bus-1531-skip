Bus 1531 SKIP stickers

Nobody from your stop riding the AM bus? Send your stop's sticker in the group so the driver knows to skip it. Find your stop, tap Download (for WhatsApp) to save it.

Download all 11 (zip)

How to use it

  1. Tap Download (for WhatsApp) under your stop. It saves a short video to your phone.
  2. In the bus group, tap attach → Photos & videos and pick it.
  3. Before sending, tap GIF at the top so it loops on its own. Then send.
  4. Next time: in the chat, long-press it and star it, so it's easy to find.
  5. WebP is the same sticker in WhatsApp's sticker format, for sticker-maker apps.
